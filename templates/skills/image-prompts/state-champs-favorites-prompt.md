# Image Prompt — "Who's Your Pick for State Champs?"

**Post concept:** Engagement post asking followers who they think will make the state championships.
**Headline:** "Who are your favorites to make the state championships?" (two-line, white + red emphasis)
**Bottom text:** "Tell us in the comments below"
**Layout:** Default (headline upper third, bottom text centered at bottom)
**Player collage:** Generic (no specific teams)

## Full Prompt

A high-energy sports marketing graphic in 9:16 vertical format for a Mississippi
high school football social media post. In the top-left corner, place the "State
Championships Radio Network" shield logo (black, white, and red football crest
design). In the top-right corner, place the "Varsity Voices — Mississippi High
School Football" microphone logo. Bold, gritty stadium-style distressed typography
dominates the upper third of the frame, reading "WHO ARE YOUR FAVORITES TO MAKE
THE" in white all-caps font, followed by "STATE CHAMPIONSHIPS?" in large red
distressed all-caps font with a rough, weathered texture. Background is a
dramatic night-time football stadium with bright stadium lights, atmospheric
haze, and blurred crowd bokeh. Arranged around the border of the frame are 4 to
8 dynamic action shots of high school football players in mid-play (running,
pointing, celebrating), each cut out and placed with a slight color-graded,
high-contrast editorial look, with small team helmet or logo icons placed near
each player. At the bottom of the frame, centered, bold white and red distressed
text reads "TELL US IN THE COMMENTS BELOW." Overall style: high-contrast, gritty,
red-black-white color palette, torn paper/spray-paint texture overlays, dramatic
lighting, editorial sports photography blended with poster-style typography.
