## Notes and deviations

- **This is not a Varsity Voices episode.** The real YouTube title is "MAIS Player of the Week-Week
  Zero-Aaric Beasley" on @StateChampRadio. The skill's `Varsity Voices | ... (S2 E## - Part N)`
  format would be wrong here, so I used a Player of the Week format instead.
- **No hosts named.** The MAIS panel is Lee Adams, Cliff Barker and Cooper Sanders, but I have no
  evidence any of them appear in this video. Naming them would be inventing. Tell me who presents
  the segment and I will add it.
- **Partner block reduced to the Guard.** MAIS partners rotate per episode and I have no deck for
  this one. The Mississippi Army National Guard is confirmed as presenting sponsor from the
  graphic. If other partners belong here, send the list and I will run them as one line with
  spaced em dashes.
- **#VarsityVoices dropped** from the hashtags, since this is not that show. Add it back if you
  want the segment associated with the podcast brand.
- **The thumbnail prompts show generic players, not Aaric.** An image model should not be asked to
  render a real high school student's likeness, and it would not get him right anyway. Since you
  already have a designed Guard graphic with his actual photo on it, that graphic is very likely
  the stronger thumbnail. Use the prompts only if you want a separate non-player-specific cover.
- All stats, the opponent and the score come straight off the graphic. Nothing was invented.
- The varsity-voices-dashboard repo is not in this connected folder, so nothing was cross-checked
  against teams.json / champs.json / players.json.
