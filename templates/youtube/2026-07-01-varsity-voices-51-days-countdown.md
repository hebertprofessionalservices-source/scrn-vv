# Varsity Voices — "51 Days" Countdown

**Premise:** With fifty-one days left until Mississippi high school football kicks back off, Varsity Voices counts down the wait and asks viewers to subscribe for every story of the season.

**Post date:** 2026-07-01
**Target length:** ~30 seconds (7 scenes)

## Visual Style Bible

- **Style:** Cinematic documentary realism, the look of a Friday Night Lights broadcast promo
- **Palette:** Deep navy and near-black skies, warm amber stadium floodlights, green turf, scattered team-color accents (no single team favored)
- **Lighting:** High-contrast stadium lighting at dusk and full night — hard beams cutting through haze, practical light flares, silhouettes against the glow
- **Camera:** Mix of slow-motion sports broadcast framing, handheld sideline energy, and wide establishing aerials; no static, sterile shots
- **Mood:** Anticipation building to excitement — quiet and still in the early scenes, kinetic and loud by the end
- **Recurring motifs:** Stadium floodlights, chalk yard lines, the number "51" appearing on a scoreboard or countdown clock, drumline brass catching light
- **Recurring character:** None — this is atmosphere-driven, no host or mascot

## Narrative Arc

Opens on quiet anticipation (empty stadium, countdown clock) → builds through sensory details of game night (turf, crowd, drumline) → escalates into full game-night energy → lands on the subscribe call-to-action and a final countdown beat.
