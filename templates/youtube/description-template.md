- Hook goes first — only ~1–2 lines show before "See more".

  matters more than clicks, put the link in the first comment instead.

## Rules

- Episode numbering: the show is in **Season 2 (2026)**; label `MAIS - S2 E## - Part N`.
  Don't trust source PDF filenames or slide episode numbers — confirm with Garret.
- Pull segments, storylines, and players from that episode's slide deck —
  never invent names or stats.
- Partners do **not** rotate. Both leagues use the same full network block, verbatim,
  on every episode and every Short — never trimmed to the deck:
  `C SPIRE — Rick's Pro Truck — Environment Masters — Entergy Mississippi — Cherokee Brick — Explore Ridgeland — Farm Families of Mississippi — Hinds Community College — Mississippi Department of Transportation — Lighthouse Limo Services — Mississippi Army National Guard — Mississippi Sports Medicine & Orthopedic — Hebert Professional Services — Case Flooring — Pine Straw America — Genuine MS — Oxford Falls`
  (The partial `[PARTNER 1] | [PARTNER 2] | [PARTNER 3]` line in the template above and
  the short lists in the S2 E01 reference examples predate this — ignore them.)
- MAIS panel is Lee Adams, Cliff Barker, Cooper Sanders. Guest co-hosts (e.g. Spence
  Shepard) get named for their episode only — never added to the standing panel.
- Week numbers are **that league's own** count, not the dashboard's overall one.
  MAIS opens two weeks earlier; in 2026 an Aug 28 MAIS video is Week 3 and an
  Aug 28 MHSAA video is Week 1. Same Friday, different label — that's correct.
- Swap `#MAIS`/`#MAISFootball` for `#MHSAA` on MHSAA episodes.
- No timestamps unless the runtime/chapter times are provided.
