## Rules

- **Keep ON THIS EPISODE short — roughly 6–9 one-line bullets.** Garret, Sep 1
  2026: "Descriptions are too long. Need to severely shorten the 'in this
  episode' section." Specifically:
  - **No per-class scoreboard dumps.** Don't write a `• 4A — ` / `• 3A — ` /
    `• 2A — ` / `• 8-Man — ` bullet listing every score in the class. Name only
    the games that got real airtime — game of the week, upsets, cross-league
    wins. The full slate lives on the dashboard, not the description.
  - **No full Top Performers roster.** A 16-name block with stat lines is far too
    much. Give the Player of the Week with their line, plus at most one or two
    others if they carried a segment.
  - **No filler bullets** — housekeeping like the MaxPreps stats plea doesn't
    earn a line.
  - Lead each bullet with its label (`Game of the Week —`, `Play of the Week —`,
    `Player of the Week —`), then the shortest true statement of what happened.
    Scores stay; commentary and qualifiers go.
  - This applies to the bullet section only. The header, intro paragraph, partner
    block, FOLLOW THE NETWORK, closing line and hashtags are unchanged.
- Episode numbering (reset by Garret Aug 31 2026 — the old `MAIS - S2 E## - Part N`
  label on this line was wrong on the part number and the shared count):
  - **Both leagues sit on Season 2.** Only the *episode* count is per-league; the
    season number is shared. Garret confirmed this again on Sep 1 2026 — an
    earlier version of this line said MAIS was Season 1, and that was wrong. Any
    `S1 E##` MAIS label is stale.
  - The two episode counts are independent — there is an Episode 2 in each — so
    never treat a number as taken because the other league used it.
  - **A season preview is `E00`**, sitting before the count. The MAIS Season
    Preview is **S2 E00**, the MAIS Week 1 recap (Aug 21 slate) is **S2 E01**, and
    the MAIS Week 2 recap (Aug 28 slate) is **S2 E02**.
  - **For MAIS, the episode number IS that week's number** (Garret, Sep 1 2026) —
    don't run an independent count, read it straight off the MAIS week the
    episode recaps. Week 2 recap = `S2 E02`, Week 3 recap = `S2 E03`. If the title
    says Week N, the code says E0N. MHSAA keeps its own sequential count.
  - **Don't renumber already-published episodes.** Garret, Sep 1 2026:
    "disregard old episodes. I don't need to change them." The rule applies to
    new work only — leave the back catalogue alone.
  - **Title format:** `Varsity Voices | [EPISODE TITLE] — S2 E##`. The episode code
    trails behind a spaced em dash; the parenthetical `(S2 E##)` form is dead.
  - **No part number unless Garret says the episode is split.**
  - **Player of the Week and Team of the Week episodes get no season/episode
    code at all** (Garret, Sep 1 2026) — the title ends after the subject. They
    sit outside the count and don't consume a number. The week number still
    applies.
  - Don't trust source PDF filenames or slide episode numbers — confirm with Garret.
- Pull segments, storylines, and players from that episode's slide deck —
  never invent names or stats.
- Partners do **not** rotate. Both leagues use the same full network block, verbatim,
  on every episode and every Short — never trimmed to the deck:
  `C SPIRE — Rick's Pro Truck — Environment Masters — Entergy Mississippi — Cherokee Brick — Explore Ridgeland — Farm Families of Mississippi — Hinds Community College — Mississippi Department of Transportation — Lighthouse Limo Services — Mississippi Army National Guard — Mississippi Sports Medicine & Orthopedic — Hebert Professional Services — Case Flooring — Pine Straw America — Genuine MS — Oxford Falls`
  (The partial `[PARTNER 1] | [PARTNER 2] | [PARTNER 3]` line in the template above and
  the short lists in the S2 E01 reference examples predate this — ignore them.)
- MAIS panel is Lee Adams, Cliff Barker, Cooper Sanders. Guest co-hosts (e.g. Spence
  Shepard) get named for their episode only — never added to the standing panel.
- Week numbers are **that league's own** count, not the dashboard's overall one.
  MAIS opens **one week earlier** (corrected by Garret Aug 31 2026 — an earlier
  version of this line said two). Weeks run Monday–Sunday: the week of Aug 24–30
  2026 is **MAIS Week 2 and MHSAA Week 1**, and the week of Aug 31–Sep 6 2026 is
  **MAIS Week 3 and MHSAA Week 2** (Garret, Sep 1 2026). Same Friday, different
  label — that's correct.
- **When a week has no settled number, label it with the date instead** (Garret,
  Aug 31 2026) — don't guess a number, don't leave it blank. The Aug 14 2026 MAIS
  slate falls before MAIS Week 1, so it is labelled "Aug 14".
- Swap `#MAIS`/`#MAISFootball` for `#MHSAA` on MHSAA episodes.
- No timestamps unless the runtime/chapter times are provided.
