# YouTube Tags Template — Varsity Voices Episodes

Hard limit: **500 characters total**, punctuation included. Always verify the
count before delivering.

Format (Garret, Aug 2026):
- Comma-separated, **no spaces after commas**
- The base list below goes on **every** video, unchanged
- Add episode-appropriate tags in the remaining budget

> **Supersedes the earlier no-brand-tags rule.** This list intentionally leads
> with SCRN brand tags, and the old "spaces after commas" formatting is gone.

## Picking the episode tags

Spend the budget on terms the base list doesn't already cover. `mississippi` and
`mississippi sports` are in the base, but the high-intent phrase
`mississippi high school football` is not — it's usually the single best addition.

Candidates by episode type:

- League: `mhsaa` (6) · `mhsaa football` (15) · `MAIS football` (14)
- Season preview: `football season preview` (24) · `high school football 2026` (26)
- In-season: `football highlights 2026` (25) · `game of the week` (17) · `top plays` (10)
- Small-school: `8 man football` (15) · `small school football` (22)
- Playoffs: `high school football playoffs` (30) · `state championship football` (28)
