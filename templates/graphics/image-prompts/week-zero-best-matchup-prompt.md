# Image Prompt — "Week Zero: Best Matchup?" (Social Engagement Post)

**Post concept:** Morning-of engagement post asking followers which MAIS Week Zero game is the best matchup (Aug. 14, 2026)
**Headline:** "WEEK ZERO" (red) + "WHAT'S THE BEST MATCHUP?" (white) — two-line red+white
**Bottom text:** "MAIS FOOTBALL · TONIGHT · TELL US BELOW"
**Layout:** Default (headline upper third, bottom text at bottom edge, open center-right for the game list overlay)
**Player collage:** Generic action shots (no specific teams)
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (swap to 1:1 or 4:5 for feed crops — see note)

## Full Prompt

A high-energy sports marketing graphic in 9:16 vertical format for a Mississippi
high school football social media post. In the top-left corner, place the "State
Championships Radio Network" shield logo (black, white, and red football shield
crest design). In the top-right corner, place the "Varsity Voices — Mississippi
High School Football" microphone logo. Bold, gritty stadium-style distressed
typography dominates the upper third, reading "WEEK ZERO" in massive crimson-red
all-caps block letters with a rough, weathered spray-paint texture, with
"WHAT'S THE BEST MATCHUP?" directly beneath in white all-caps distressed font at
about half the size. Background is a dramatic night-time football stadium with
bright stadium lights flaring, heavy atmospheric haze, and blurred red-tinted
crowd bokeh. Arranged around the left and right borders of the frame are 4 to 6
dynamic cut-out action shots of generic high school football players in mid-play
— a quarterback mid-throw, a running back breaking a tackle, a receiver
stretching for a catch, a defender celebrating — each with a bold white outline
stroke and punchy high-contrast editorial color grading, angled inward so they
frame an open, darker vertical space through the center of the composition where
a matchup list will be overlaid later. Keep that center channel clean and
uncluttered. At the bottom edge, a red banner strip with bold white distressed
all-caps text reads "MAIS FOOTBALL · TONIGHT · TELL US BELOW." Overall style:
high-contrast, gritty, red-black-white color palette, torn paper/spray-paint
texture overlays, dramatic rim lighting, editorial sports photography blended
with poster-style typography.

## Production notes

- **Do not have the AI render the 8-game list** — generators mangle long text.
  The prompt deliberately leaves a clean center channel; drop the matchups in
  afterward in Canva/Photoshop with the brand font so every school name is correct.
- **Aspect ratios:** generate 9:16 for TikTok/Stories/Reels. For the Facebook,
  Instagram, and X feed versions, re-run with "1:1 square format" or "4:5 vertical
  format" — the framing (headline top, players on the borders, open center) survives
  all three crops.
- Two different Lee Academys play Week Zero (Colts/Clarksdale MS and Cougars/
  Marianna AR) — label them distinctly in the overlaid list.
