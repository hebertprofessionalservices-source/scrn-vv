# Hinds Community College — Sponsor Promo Thumbnail Prompts
Co-branded SCRN + Hinds. Subject: high-school-to-Hinds athletics pipeline.

Attach two logo files when generating: the SCRN shield and the Hinds Community College
horizontal lockup.

Design choices worth knowing:
- Palette shifted from SCRN red to Hinds maroon so the sponsor colour leads. SCRN red and
  Hinds maroon sitting side by side would read as a mistake.
- "TO HINDS" is maroon with a white outline stroke. Maroon type on a dark stadium background
  has poor contrast without it.
- The college-side uniform is plain maroon and white, generic, with no Hinds marks on it.
  Only the attached logo carries the brand.
