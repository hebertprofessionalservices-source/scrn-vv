## Before you use these

- **C Spire is not on this one.** You asked for three specific logos, and the Guard is the
  presenting sponsor of Player of the Week. That conflicts with the standing rule to put C Spire
  on every thumbnail. Tell me which way you want it and I will make the skill match. Adding a
  fourth logo to a 1280x720 frame will make all four small, so it is worth deciding deliberately.
- **YouTube playlist covers work differently from video thumbnails.** YouTube has been rolling
  out custom playlist thumbnails, but availability and image requirements have changed more than
  once and I could not confirm the current rules from Google's own help page. Check YouTube Studio
  before assuming you can upload this directly. The reliable fallback is to set this image as the
  thumbnail of the video you want fronting the playlist, since the playlist inherits it.
- Players are generic. No real athlete's likeness should be generated.
