# Image Prompt — "The MAIS 4A Sleeper" (YouTube Short Cover, 9:16 Vertical)

**Post concept:** Vertical cover for a YouTube Short on the 2026 MAIS 4A sleeper team
**YouTube title:** The 2026 MAIS 4A Sleeper Team You Need to Watch!
**Headline:** "THE MAIS 4A" (white) + "SLEEPER" (red emphasis)
**Bottom text:** Banner strip: "MAIS 4A · 2026 PREDICTIONS"
**Layout:** Logos top, stacked headline upper third, single focal player centre/lower, banner above the bottom safe zone
**Focal subject:** Lone running back emerging from shadow into a hard spotlight — visual shorthand for "overlooked team nobody's watching", and a nod to the episode's run-game thesis
**Accent prop:** None. Parts 1 and 2 both used the gold trophy; a sleeper story isn't a trophy story, and vertical needs the breathing room
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920)

## Shorts-specific notes

Unlike the TikTok cover, the Shorts player overlays UI on top of the frame:

- Right edge (~12%) — like / comment / share / remix buttons
- Bottom (~15%) — channel handle, title, description
- Top-right — occasionally the Shorts menu

So the focal subject sits centre-left, the banner rides **above** the bottom safe
zone rather than on the very edge, and nothing important touches the right edge.

## Full Prompt

A high-energy vertical cover image in 9:16 portrait format (1080x1920) for a
Mississippi high school football predictions show, composed for vertical with
nothing stretched or distorted. At the very top, side by side: the "State
Championships Radio Network" shield logo (black, white, and red football crest)
in the top-left corner and the "Varsity Voices — Mississippi High School
Football" microphone logo in the top-right corner. In the upper third of the
frame, bold gritty stadium-style distressed typography stacked and centered:
"THE MAIS 4A" in huge white all-caps spray-paint-textured font, above "SLEEPER"
in even larger crimson-red distressed all-caps font with rough weathered edges —
oversized lettering that stays readable at small preview size. Filling the
middle and lower-centre of the frame, one dominant cut-out of a generic high
school running back in a plain unmarked white and navy uniform, carrying the
football tucked in one arm and cutting hard upfield, emerging out of deep shadow
into a single hard shaft of stadium light so that half his body is rim-lit and
half falls into darkness, rendered in the cut-out-with-white-outline style with
punchy high-contrast editorial color grading. Position him centre-left so the
right edge of the frame stays clear. Background is a dramatic night-time
football stadium with bright flaring lights, heavy atmospheric haze, red-tinted
blurred crowd bokeh, and dark grunge texture. Set slightly above the bottom edge,
a red banner strip with bold white distressed all-caps text reading "MAIS 4A ·
2026 PREDICTIONS." Keep the headline, the player's head and the banner within
the centre-safe area, leaving the bottom 15% and right 12% of the frame free of
important content so nothing is hidden behind player controls. Overall style:
high-contrast, gritty, red-black-white palette, torn paper/spray-paint texture
overlays, dramatic rim lighting, editorial sports photography blended with
poster-style typography.

Important: the player must be entirely generic — no readable text of any kind on
the jersey, helmet, or scoreboard, no school names or mascots, no brand logos
(no Nike swoosh, no Riddell or other manufacturer marks), and no recognizable
face. Plain solid-colour uniform with no wordmark across the chest.
