# Image Prompt — Varsity Voices Horizontal Logo (1200×630)

**Post concept:** Reformat the Varsity Voices crest into a wide 1.91:1 social/lockup logo, keeping every brand element from the original
**Layout:** Emblem (football + microphone crest) on the left, stacked wordmark + banner on the right
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9-ish social ratio — 1200×630 px (1.91:1)

## Full Prompt

A professional horizontal sports logo lockup, 1200x630 pixels, wide 1.91:1
format, for "Varsity Voices," a Mississippi high school football broadcast show.
Reformat an existing square crest logo into a horizontal layout while keeping
every brand characteristic identical. On the left third, a circular emblem: a
red-and-white striped leather football with white laces, angled diagonally, with
a gleaming chrome vintage broadcast microphone standing in front of it, tilted
slightly, framed by a navy-blue shield outline with a thin metallic gold trim.
On the right two-thirds, the stacked wordmark: "VARSITY" in bold italic
condensed white block letters with a navy-blue outline, sitting on an angled
navy banner, and directly beneath it "VOICES" in even larger bold italic white
block letters with a crimson-red outline and subtle 3D bevel — both words in the
same aggressive collegiate athletic block font as the original crest. Below the
wordmark, a long navy-blue ribbon banner with thin gold edging reads
"MISSISSIPPI HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL" in clean white all-caps sans-serif
letterspaced type. Color palette strictly limited to: navy blue, crimson red,
white, metallic gold trim accents, and chrome silver for the microphone. Glossy
modern sports-crest style with subtle bevels and highlights, crisp vector-like
edges, balanced margins on all sides so nothing touches the frame edge, on a
solid dark maroon-to-black gradient background. No extra text, no players, no
stadium — logo lockup only.
