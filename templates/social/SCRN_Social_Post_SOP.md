# SCRN Social Media Post SOP

## Title Format

A short, punchy hook followed by a reaction or challenge. End with 1–2 emojis (use two when both add something; one is fine when a second would feel forced).

**Example:**
> Our Top 10 List Is Out. Come At Us 😤🏈

---

## Post Body Format

**1. Description (2–3 sentences)**
Write a short, conversational description of the clip. Keep it human — no AI-sounding phrases. Reference who's speaking and what they said or did. End with something that teases the reaction or punchline.

**Example:**
> Jared's not losing sleep over this one. Neville Barr is on the list, and the numbers are right there. Don't like it? That's on Davis.

---

**2. Watch Link**
> Watch the full [episode/segment title] here: [URL]

**Example:**
> Watch the full Top 10 Mississippi HS Football Coaches countdown here: https://youtu.be/h-YprQx2zuU

---

**3. Platform Line**
> 📲 @StateChampRadio
> YouTube | Facebook | Instagram | TikTok | Twitter | Twitch | Threads

---

**4. Hashtags**
Use a maximum of 5 hashtags. Choose the most viral, actively searched tags relevant to the specific post. Do NOT use brand hashtags like #VarsityVoices or #SportsChampionshipsRadio — the account is still growing and brand tags won't drive discovery. Pick tags people actually search for.

Good examples: #HighSchoolFootball #MississippiFootball #MSFootball #Top10 #FootballCoach #MHSAA

**Example:**
> #MSFootball #HighSchoolFootball #Top10 #FootballCoach #MHSAA
