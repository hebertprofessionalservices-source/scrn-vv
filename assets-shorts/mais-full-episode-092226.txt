The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Lee Adams and Co-Hosts Cliff Barker and Cooper Sanders are joined by guest Bryan Eubank, the voice of Jackson Academy, to break down a loaded MAIS Week 5 and preview a Week 6 headlined by a rematch of last year's 3A Division I state championship.

ON THIS EPISODE
• Game of the Week — Tri-County at Leake Academy, a rematch of last year's 3A Division I title game (Leake won 25-24)
• Player of the Week — Heritage Academy's Joshua "Juice" Tate: over 500 total yards and six TDs in a 44-36 comeback win over Leake Academy, snapping the team's 0-4 start
• Madison-Ridgeland Academy stuns Jackson Prep 28-25 on a gutsy 4th-and-4 call, emerging in a loaded 4A field with Prep, Jackson Academy, and Hartfield
• Columbia stays unbeaten and unscored-on, blanking Silliman 42-0; Central Hinds rolls past St. Aloysius 49-14 in 3A
• Canton Academy climbs the 2A rankings after learning from its Week 1 loss to Tri-County, beating Winston Academy 36-19
• Pick'em — Cliff and Lee both go 7-for-9, Cooper 6-for-9, and guest picker Bryan Eubank joins the board for Week 6
