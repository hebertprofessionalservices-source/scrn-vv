DESCRIPTION
Varsity Voices | MAIS Week 3 Player of the Week — Wade Smith

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Cooper Sanders sits down with Jackson Prep senior running back Wade Smith, the Mississippi Army National Guard MAIS Player of the Week.

ON THIS SEGMENT
• Wade Smith goes for 231 rushing yards and four touchdowns — three rushing, one receiving — in Jackson Prep's 38-24 win over Parklane
• All four scores came after halftime, after Parklane stacked the box and shut the run down early
• Smith on his offensive line: he's played with the core group since fifth grade, and they're getting tomahawk steaks at his house if he reaches 2,000 yards
• Twenty pounds added in the offseason and no more splitting carries — 419 rushing yards through two games
• Jackson Prep keeps every week's rankings on a locker room billboard: "we take it personal"
• Team motto is go 1-0 every week, and Copiah Academy is next

This segment is brought to you by the Mississippi Army National Guard.
