Standing Room Only — Kosciusko Packed Both Sides | Varsity Voices

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Breck Riley and Co-Hosts Jared Shotts, Brandon Davis, and Jake Wimberly on what Week 1 looked like from the stands — a Kosciusko crowd that filled both sidelines and a state that turned out everywhere.

IN THIS CLIP
• Breck called the Kosciusko game standing room only — both sidelines full, in Week 1
• Kosciusko beat Neshoba Central 55-35 at home
• Neshoba Central travels about 40 minutes; Breck: "insanely packed"
• Breck notes seven million watched the NFL Hall of Fame game the same week
• A strong Thursday-night JUCO crowd too, Pearl River vs Holmes
