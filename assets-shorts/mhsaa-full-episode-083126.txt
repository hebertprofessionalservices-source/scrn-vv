Varsity Voices | MHSAA Week 1 Team of the Week — Meridian's Tony Vance

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. In this special edition, Brandon Davis sits down with Meridian head coach Tony Vance after the Wildcats opened the season with a 47-9 win over Ocean Springs — the result that made them our Mississippi Sports Medicine Team of the Week.

ON THIS EPISODE
• Mississippi Sports Medicine Team of the Week — Meridian, 47-9 over Ocean Springs on Aug 27. Nearly 200 yards passing and roughly 300 rushing in a balanced night, with the defense forcing a three-and-out on the opening series.
• Coach Tony Vance on returning to Mississippi — 17 years at Hattiesburg, a December 2024 retirement, and what pulled him back to Ray Stadium and the Meridian tradition.
• Coaching his son — Deuce Vance, Mr. 6A Football, now at South Alabama, and the film sessions his wife shut down at the dinner table.
• Building the staff — defensive coordinator Rashad Gaines, plus former head coaches added on that side of the ball.
• Players to Watch —
  Chase Gathings  WR · SR · Meridian · 6-6, 200 — big night receiving, then blocked an extra point with the game long decided
  Grant Hill  RB · JR · Meridian
  Cordarrin Wilson  QB · JR · Meridian · 9-of-11 passing
  Jadarius Williams  WR · SR · Meridian — big night receiving
  Ashton Dawson  LB · SR · Meridian
• A surprise drop-in from Petal's Marcus Boyles, fresh off a 35-34 win over Hattiesburg decided by an extra point.
• "Rat poison" — how Vance reset a locker room after an opening-week statement, with Neshoba Central next and Starkville, Columbus and Hattiesburg to follow.

Broadcasting from the Environment Masters Studio — hassle-free plumbing and AC.
