DESCRIPTION
Varsity Voices | MAIS Week 2 Player of the Week — Ethan Butler

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Cliff Barker sits down with East Rankin Academy junior running back Ethan Butler, the Army National Guard MAIS Player of the Week.

ON THIS SEGMENT
• Ethan Butler goes for 282 yards and 5 touchdowns on just 10 carries in East Rankin's 50-12 win over Acadiana Christian in MAIS Week 2
• Butler breaks down his outside-speed running style and a personal-best 4.47 forty
• Credit where it's due — Butler on the offensive line: "they've helped so much"
• A look ahead to East Rankin's Week 3 matchup with Canton Academy, and a bold prediction from Cliff
• Head coach Teddy Dyess has East Rankin off to a 2-0 start at 4A

This segment is brought to you by the Mississippi Army National Guard.
