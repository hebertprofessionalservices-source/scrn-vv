Varsity Voices | MAIS 2026 Week 2 Recap — 4A Through 8-Man — S2 E02

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Lee Adams and Co-Hosts Cliff Barker and Cooper Sanders, joined by guest co-host Adam Northam, the voice of the Brookhaven Cougars and the Co-Lin Wolves, recap MAIS Week 2 — Jackson Prep's 38-24 game-of-the-week win over Parklane, a 231-yard, four-touchdown night from our Player of the Week, Hartfield and Madison-Ridgeland both taking down MHSAA opponents, and a one-second Hail Mary in Winona — from 4A all the way down to eight-man.

ON THIS EPISODE
• Game of the Week — Jackson Prep 38, Parklane 24
• Two MAIS wins over MHSAA teams — Hartfield 42, Columbia 28 and Madison-Ridgeland 30, Jefferson Davis County 27
• Upsets — Benton Academy 46, Columbus Christian 14 and Canton Academy 20, East Rankin 14
• Play of the Week — Winona Christian's one-second Hail Mary to beat Marshall Academy 16-14
• Why 3A is the deepest race on the board
• MAIS in the NFL — Swayze makes the Bengals' 53-man roster
• Player of the Week — Wade Smith, Jackson Prep: 26 carries, 231 yds, 4 TD
• Pick'em results and the Week 3 board
