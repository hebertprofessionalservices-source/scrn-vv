The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Breck Riley and Co-Hosts Jake Wimberly and Brandon Davis (Jared Shotts is out this week) recap a wild Friday of MHSAA football — a 60-58 double-overtime classic in Kosciusko, Warren Central's playoff-atmosphere win at Germantown, and West Point's first 0-3 start since 2000 — from 7A down to 1A.

ON THIS EPISODE
• Game of the Week — Kosciusko outlasts Hernando 60-58 in double overtime, trading two-point conversions to the final snap
• Warren Central wins a playoff-atmosphere thriller at Germantown, 42-35 — Nash Morgan becomes the first player in Warren County history to pass for 30 touchdowns and rush for 30 in a career
• Biggest storyline — West Point falls to 0-3 for the first time since 2000 after a 41-28 loss to Noxubee County
• Battle of the Bell — Greene County knocks off 6A George County 21-7 to stay unbeaten at 3-0
• Play of the Week — a fumble returned 103 yards for a touchdown swings the Warren Central-Germantown game
• Player of the Week — Kosciusko's Kaden Wade: 24-of-40 passing for 306 yards and 4 TDs, plus 65 rushing yards and 2 more scores (371 total yards, 6 total TDs) in the double-OT win
• Pass Christian outlasts Presbyterian Christian 40-38 in a 4A shootout behind Paris Trivillion's three touchdown passes
