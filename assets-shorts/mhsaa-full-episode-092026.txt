• Game of the Week — Petal 40, Gulfport 39 (OT). Caden Belton leads a no-timeout drive to tie it with 21 seconds left, then Petal goes for two in overtime and Belton converts

• Warren Central 45, Vicksburg 3 in the River City rivalry
