The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Cliff Barker sits down with Starkville Academy senior linebacker Brennan Templeton, the Mississippi Army National Guard MAIS Player of the Week.

ON THIS SEGMENT
• Templeton piles up 20 tackles, including 2 for loss, in a tough loss to Pillow Academy
• Credits his defensive line for occupying blockers: "they open up plenty of opportunities for us linebackers to make the plays"
• A downhill, contact-seeking linebacker: "anything up the middle, it's usually my part of the game"
• Lifelong Starkville kid gearing up for this week's rivalry game with Winston, "right down the road"
• Committed to Holmes Community College to play baseball (third base) next year
