Varsity Voices | MAIS 2026 Week 3 Recap / Week 4 Preview — MRA vs. Hartfield — S2 E04

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Cliff Barker fills in for an absent Lee Adams (dealing with storm power outages down south), joined by Co-Host Cooper Sanders and guest co-host Phillip Palmertree of MRA, to recap MAIS Week 3 — MRA's 42-21 statement win over Hartfield — and set the table for Week 4.

ON THIS EPISODE
• Game of the Week — Madison-Ridgeland Academy 42, Hartfield 21, behind Colson O'Cain's return from injury (26 carries, 169 yards, 1 TD) and a big night from senior QB Austin Banks III
• Running back showcase — Garrett Adcock goes 20-279-4 in Leake's 48-6 win over Winston, and Cade Hunt piles up 222 yards on just six carries in Tri-County's 50-7 win over Madison St. Joseph
• The panel pushes back on the "MAIS is an inferior league" talk, pointing to Hartfield, JA, Jackson Prep and MRA's long track record against MHSAA opponents
• 3A shakes up at the top — Tri-County jumps to #1, Leake to #2, ACCS to #3, with Washington, Lamar and Columbia rounding out the crew's top five
• 2A heating up — Oak Hill stays unbeaten at #1, Canton escapes Indianola 22-20, and Magnolia Heights blanks Heritage 40-0
• 8-man chaos — Calhoun's 72-32 rout of North Sunflower, a wild 56-54 Tunica win over Delta Academy, and another rough week for Columbus Christian
• Player of the Week — Brennan Templeton, Starkville Academy, presented by the Mississippi Army National Guard
• Week 4 picks — Jackson Prep at Tri-County headlines a full slate of MAIS-MHSAA crossover games
