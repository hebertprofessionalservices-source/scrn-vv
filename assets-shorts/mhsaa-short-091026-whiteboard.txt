IN THIS CLIP
• Brandon's top five: 5. Pink Floyd, 4. The Who, 3. Black Sabbath, 2. The Rolling Stones, 1. Led Zeppelin
• The Beatles don't crack his top 50: "I don't like the Beatles"
• Jared agrees on ditching the Beatles
