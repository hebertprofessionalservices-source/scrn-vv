DESCRIPTION
Two Legends, One Screen — Meridian, Team of the Week | Varsity Voices

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Breck Riley and Co-Hosts Jared Shotts, Brandon Davis, and Jake Wimberly name Meridian the MHSAA Week 1 Team of the Week — and the interview with head coach Tony Vance picks up an uninvited second legend.

IN THIS CLIP
• Meridian is the Week 1 Team of the Week after a 47-9 opener at Ocean Springs
• Head coach Tony Vance joins Brandon Davis, and Petal head coach Marcus Boyles turns up unannounced to say hey to a longtime friend
• The two go back roughly 12 years through the Petal-Hattiesburg rivalry
• Boyles on the extra point that beat Hattiesburg 35-34: "Divine intervention"
• They will see each other again on October 30, when Meridian travels to Petal
