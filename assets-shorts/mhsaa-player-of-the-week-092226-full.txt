The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Jared Shotts sits down with Winona senior running back Yoshawn Hudson, the Mississippi Army National Guard MHSAA Player of the Week, off a blowout win over Houston.

ON THIS SEGMENT
• Hudson erupts for 130 rush yards on just 5 carries (26.0 per carry), 3 rushing TDs, a kickoff-return TD, and a two-point conversion in a 50-26 win over Houston
• Season numbers: 29 carries, 657 yards, 10 TDs — a 22.7 yards-per-carry average as Winona improves to 4-0
• On the team's chemistry: "we got a deep relationship... been playing with each other since Pee-Wee"
• Fuel from last year's North State Championship loss: "we wasn't doing the little things right"
• Committed to Mississippi State and Jeff Lebby, staying focused heading into region play: "take care of my body... do the little things right"
