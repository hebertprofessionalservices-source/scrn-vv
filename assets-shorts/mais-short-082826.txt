DESCRIPTION
5 Touchdowns on 10 Carries — Ethan Butler Was Unreal | Varsity Voices

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Lee Adams and Co-Host Cliff Barker sit down with East Rankin Academy senior RB Ethan Butler after one of the most efficient stat lines of the season.

IN THIS CLIP
• Ethan Butler goes 282 rushing yards and 5 touchdowns on just 10 carries
• Cliff Barker calls it one of the best stat lines he's seen — the carry-to-touchdown ratio is the headline
• Butler credits his offensive line: "they got me to where I needed to be"
• His approach in the open field: find the end zone, every time
