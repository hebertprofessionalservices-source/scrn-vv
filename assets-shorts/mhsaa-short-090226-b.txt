The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Brandon Davis previews Friday's Clarkdale-Stringer meeting, a 1-0 Bulldogs team coming off 56 points against an 0-1 Red Devils side with a coach who knows how to build one.

IN THIS CLIP
• Clarkdale opened with a 56-15 win over Resurrection Catholic
• Jake Lee went 3-of-4 for 133 yards and three touchdowns — three completions, three scores
• Leland Harris ran seven times for 136 yards, 19.4 a carry
• Stringer fell 40-24 at Enterprise and hits the road again Friday
• Brandon on Red Devils coach Dan Brady: a state title at Bay Springs, where he went 99-35 across ten seasons
• Stringer at Clarkdale, Friday night
