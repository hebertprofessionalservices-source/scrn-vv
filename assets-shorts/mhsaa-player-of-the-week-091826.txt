Varsity Voices | MHSAA Week 3 Player of the Week — Kosciusko's Caiden Wade

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Jared Shotts sits down with Kosciusko senior quarterback Caiden Wade, the Mississippi Army National Guard MHSAA Player of the Week, off a double-overtime classic over 7A Hernando.

ON THIS SEGMENT
• Wade goes 24-of-48 for 306 yards and 4 TDs, then adds 65 rushing yards and 2 more scores — 371 total yards, 6 TDs — in a 60-58 double-overtime win over Hernando
• Kosciusko improves to 3-0 behind a veteran offense carrying a defense replacing nine or ten starters
• On the mindset in a shootout: "doing whatever it takes to win, no matter what it was"
• Fuel from last season: a 6-0 loss to Columbia in the 4A state title game — "we couldn't even put up a touchdown"
• Committed to Southern Miss, where OC-turned-head-coach Blake Anderson has been in his corner "since day one"
• Superstitions: a haircut every Thursday and the same socks every Friday
• Sets up Friday's test against 6A Olive Branch
