The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Hosts Jared Shotts and Brandon Davis hold the show down solo this week (Host Breck Riley and Co-Host Jake Wimberly are out) and preview a loaded Week 3 slate from 7A down to 1A, plus their picks for every headline matchup.

ON THIS EPISODE
• Game of the Week — Pass Christian's Paris Trivillion and Presbyterian Christian's Smith Stringer headline a 4A quarterback duel between two 2-0 teams
• Oak Grove looks to bounce back against rival Hattiesburg, fresh off the Tigers' Little Brown Jug win over Laurel
• Warren Central travels to Germantown, where the Mavericks try to end an 0-7 series history against the Vikings
• Must-Win — winless West Point (0-2, 7 points a game) hosts battle-tested Noxubee County (2-0) to avoid an 0-3 start
• Upset Alert — 2-0 D'Iberville hosts 3-0 Seminary and QB Brady Chancellor, already past 400 passing yards on the year
• Louisville hosts national power John Curtis out of Louisiana
• LaFleur County's defense (3 points a game allowed) hosts South Delta and freshman QB Jeremy Pollard
• Defending 2A champ East Webster hosts Biggersville
• Whiteboard — Brandon ranks his top five British rock bands (no Beatles), plus picks for every game on the board
