The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. ON THIS EPISODE
• Game of the Week — Madison-Ridgeland Academy holds off Collierville (TN) 31-21, improving to 3-1
• Jackson Prep stays unbeaten at 4-0, routing Tri-County 42-12 behind a big night from Luke Essery and receivers Asher Reynolds and Jay Bennett
• Washington keeps rising in 3A, beating rival Greenville St. Joseph 34-18
• Lamar falls to Caledonia 31-20 despite a big night from Sullivan Reed
• Reminder — 8-Man is officially one division this year; the show's graphics are still catching up and showing it split into two slides
