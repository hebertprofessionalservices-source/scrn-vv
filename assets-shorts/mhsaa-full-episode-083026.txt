Varsity Voices | MHSAA 2026 Week 1 Recap — 7A Through 1A (S2 E01)

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Breck Riley and Co-Hosts Jared Shotts and Brandon Davis recap the opening Friday of the MHSAA season — Meridian's 47-9 statement at Ocean Springs, Brett Morgan's 17-15 debut win at Starkville over Oak Grove, and a 478-yard, five-touchdown night from our Player of the Week — with every classification from 7A down to 1A.
