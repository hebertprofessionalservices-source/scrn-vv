DESCRIPTION
Varsity Voices | MHSAA Week 1 Player of the Week — Jaylin Stowers

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Brandon Davis sits down with Morton junior quarterback Jaylin Stowers and head coach Zach Jones, after a 478-yard, five-touchdown night in the Panthers' 42-13 win over Newton County.

ON THIS SEGMENT
• Jaylin Stowers throws for 318 yards and three scores on 19-of-23 passing, runs for 160 and two more, and adds an interception at safety
• Coach Jones on the tape: about as close to perfect as you can get, with a 70-plus-yard run called back on top of it
• Stowers has started since his freshman year; Jones on what changed — the leadership, not the arm
• Why Jones is reluctant to take a Division I safety off the field, and where he projects at the next level
• Credit to offensive coordinator and quarterback coach Elijah McDonald for the board work and film study
• Morton travels to West Lauderdale next, a team they beat twice last season
