DESCRIPTION
A 4.47 Forty — Ethan Butler Has Serious Wheels | Varsity Voices

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Cliff Barker asks East Rankin Academy junior running back Ethan Butler the question everyone's asking after his 5-touchdown night: just how fast is he?

IN THIS CLIP
• Ethan Butler's last recorded 40-yard dash: 4.47 seconds
• Cliff Barker on what that means for his game: an outside-running back who's a "terror" once he clears the second level
• Fresh off 282 yards and 5 touchdowns on 10 carries in East Rankin's Week 2 win over Acadiana Christian
