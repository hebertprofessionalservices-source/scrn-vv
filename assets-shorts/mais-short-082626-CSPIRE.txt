Jackson Prep led 28-0 in the second quarter. It ended 42-35. Lee Adams explains.

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. In MAIS Week 2, Jackson Prep hosted UMS Wright in Flowood — the Alabama prep program founded back in 1893 — and built a four-touchdown lead before the visitors turned it into a one-score game.

IN THIS CLIP
• Jackson Prep 42, UMS Wright 35 — 28-0 in the second quarter, 28-14 by halftime
• A scoreless third quarter, then 35 combined points in the fourth
• Wade Smith (RB, Sr.) — over 100 yards rushing and three touchdowns
• Herrin Goodman (RB, Jr.) — over 100 yards rushing
• Luke Essary (QB, Sr.) — 7-of-15 passing for over 100 yards
• Where the name "UMS Wright" comes from — Dr. Wright's 1893 school, the girls' preparatory school that followed in the 1920s, and the 1988 merger

FILE NAMING CONVENTION — assets-shorts/
===============================================================================

  <league>-short-MMDDYY.txt

League is "mais" or "mhsaa", lowercase. The date is the PUBLISH date, not the
game date. Both are recorded in the header block of each file.

  mais-short-082826.txt    this file
  mhsaa-short-MMDDYY.txt   MHSAA shorts, same folder

Week numbers in the assets are that league's OWN week, not the overall count.
MAIS opens two weeks before MHSAA, so an Aug 28 MAIS video is Week 3 and an
Aug 28 MHSAA video is Week 1. Same Friday, different label — that is correct.
