The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Breck Riley and Co-Hosts Jared Shotts and Brandon Davis (Jake Wimberly is out this week) preview a loaded Week 4 slate — two Top 10 7A showdowns, a 15th straight for Warren Central, and an upset alert in 3A — from 7A down to 1A.

ON THIS EPISODE
• Two Top 10 showdowns lead 7A — #1 Tupelo hosts #7 Oxford (both 3-0), while unbeaten Petal meets Gulfport's shutout defense in South Mississippi
• New segment debut — Team of the Week honors the Leake County Alligators and Coach Kenneth Whitfield after their breakout win
• Warren Central goes for a 15th straight win in the rivalry with Vicksburg
• Old rivals renew it — South Panola and Clarksdale headline a 6A throwback that used to be the game of the week
• Upset alert — Mize, coming off a one-point loss to McGee, gets a real shot (58%) against defending 3A champ Raleigh
• Rivalry week across the state — the County Line Clash (Belmont-Tishomingo County) and the Possum Bowl (Lloyd Star-Bogue Chitto) headline the smaller classes
• Lafayette County at Northside is a virtual coin flip (51%) in the week's best 1A-3A crossover
