Jared Shotts sits down with the Mississippi Army National Guard Player of the Week, McComb Tigers wide receiver turned quarterback Sheldon Isaac, after a six-touchdown night in McComb's win over Hazlehurst.

ON THIS EPISODE
• Player of the Week — Sheldon Isaac, McComb: 13-of-22 for 249 yards and three touchdowns, plus 114 rushing yards and three touchdowns on 10 carries
• McComb 43, Hazlehurst 21
• Moving from wide receiver to quarterback after the starter was hurt, having played quarterback in junior high
• His South Florida commitment as a receiver
• What a receiver's eyes bring to playing quarterback
• Next up: McComb at Poplarville and Ty Keys, with the game moved up to Thursday, October 1
• Isaac thanks his mother, his coaches and the older brother who got him into football
