The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Host Breck Riley and Co-Hosts Brandon Davis and Jake Wimberly break down Canton's statement 47-40 road win over Terry.

IN THIS CLIP
• Canton goes on the road and beats Terry 47-40, a result Breck and Jake call a statement win
• Jake Wimberly: throwing 47 on Terry's defense is impressive — a group that returned a lot of starters this year
• Jake still likes what Coach Kris Thigpen is building at Terry, calling them a playoff team regardless of the loss
• Canton improves to 2-0; Terry falls to 1-1
• Both are 6A programs — Canton in Region 2, Terry in Region 3 — so this was a non-region measuring-stick game
