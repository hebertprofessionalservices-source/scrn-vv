Varsity Voices | MHSAA Week 2 Player of the Week — Paris Trivillion

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Jared Shotts sits down with Pass Christian senior quarterback Paris Trivillion, the Mississippi Army National Guard MHSAA Player of the Week, off a two-way explosion in a win over Purvis.

ON THIS SEGMENT
• Trivillion goes 17-of-30 for 260 yards and 2 TD through the air, then adds 256 yards and 4 more scores on just 9 carries in a 55-19 win over Purvis
• Pass Christian is 2-0, outscoring opponents 103-22 and allowing just 11 points a game
• On the added rushing production this season: "coach tells me if nothing's there, beat with your legs"
• All-senior offensive line — Hunter Boling, Andrew Murray, George Keller and more — earned a free team lunch from O-line coach Stockstill after a 400-yard rushing game on Labor Day
• Also starts at safety and pulls scout-team reps to prep the Pirates' defense for this week's air raid attack
• Sets up Friday's 2-0 vs. 2-0 showdown with Presbyterian Christian and QB Smith Stringer
• On his senior-year goal: "the best season in program history"
