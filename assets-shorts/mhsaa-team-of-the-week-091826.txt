Varsity Voices | MHSAA Week 3 Team of the Week — Leake Central Gators

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Host Jared Shotts sits down with Leake Central head coach Kenneth Whitfield, whose Gators are the Mississippi Sports Medicine MHSAA Team of the Week after opening 3-0.

ON THIS SEGMENT
• Leake Central improves to 3-0 — a 35-0 shutout of Provine, a 34-18 win over Vicksburg, and a 59-26 rout of Holmes County
• Davion Townsend does it all: 7-of-9 passing for 140 yards and 2 TDs, 9 carries for 141 yards and 2 more TDs, plus a 43-yard pick-six against Holmes County
• Coach Whitfield on leading the newly consolidated Leake Central/Leake County program: "winning cures all... we built it through hard work and raising the level of expectation"
• A coaching tree running through Louisville, a JUCO stop under Coach Underwood, Delta State under Ryan Roberts, and stints at Ole Miss and Forest Hill
• Other key contributors: running back Michelle LaFleur, linebacker Grant Allen, Richard Harris, Damarian Moore and the offensive line
• Next up — Friday night against Lanier and Coach Tommy Kelly, with Heidelberg and Cleveland Central still to come
