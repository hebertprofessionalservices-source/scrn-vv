63 Points in Week 1 — Nobody Sees Gautier Coming | Varsity Voices

The State Championships Radio Network presents Varsity Voices — your home for Mississippi high school football. Co-Hosts Brandon Davis and Jared Shotts get to Gautier at D'Iberville, two 1-0 teams on Friday's board, and Brandon is taking the Gators.

IN THIS CLIP
• Gautier hung 63 on George County in a 63-35 opener
• D'Iberville opened with a 21-6 win at Raleigh — both sides come in 1-0
• Brandon on the Gators: they can run it and throw it, and nobody wants them later in the season
• "A team down on the coast that a lot of folks don't know about"
• Brandon notes Gautier played for a state title a few years back — they went four deep into the 2024 playoffs, beating Brookhaven 37-35 on November 29
• Gautier at D'Iberville, Friday night
